Department of Computer Science and Engineering (CSE)

The CSE department started in 2004. It is the largest department of Riverbend University, with about 1,900 students and 62 teachers. The Head of the Department is Dr. Farhana Rahman.

Programs

  • BSc in CSE: 4 years, 148 credits.
  • MSc in CSE: 1.5 years, 36 credits, with a thesis or project.

Labs

The department has 7 computer labs with about 300 computers in total. Special labs include the Artificial Intelligence Lab, the Networking Lab, the Robotics Lab and the IoT Lab. Labs are open to students from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM, Sunday to Thursday.

Final year project

Every BSc student must complete a final year project (6 credits) in a group of up to 3 students. The project runs for two trimesters. Students choose a supervisor in the 10th trimester.

Clubs

The Riverbend Programming Club runs weekly contest practice every Saturday at 10:00 AM in Lab 3. The Riverbend AI Society organizes a yearly hackathon in November.

Internship

An internship of at least 3 months is required in the final year. The department helps students find internships with partner companies such as software firms in Dhaka and Chattogram.
