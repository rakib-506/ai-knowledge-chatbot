ADMISSIONS GUIDE - RIVERBEND UNIVERSITY

Who can apply
Students who passed HSC (or A Levels) can apply for undergraduate programs. The minimum requirement is a combined SSC and HSC GPA of 7.0, with at least GPA 3.0 in each exam. For A Level students, the minimum is 5 O Level subjects and 2 A Level subjects with at least grade C.

Extra requirements by program
- CSE and EEE: students must have Mathematics and Physics in HSC (or A Level).
- Law: students must pass a written test and a short interview.
- MBA: a four-year bachelor's degree with a CGPA of at least 2.50 out of 4.00. Two years of job experience is preferred but not required.

Admission test
All undergraduate applicants take an admission test. The test is 60 minutes long and has 60 multiple-choice questions on English, Mathematics and General Knowledge. There is no negative marking. The pass mark is 40 out of 60.

Deadlines
- Spring trimester: apply by 15 December. Admission test on the first Friday of January.
- Summer trimester: apply by 15 April. Admission test on the first Friday of May.
- Fall trimester: apply by 15 August. Admission test on the first Friday of September.
Late applications are not accepted.

How to apply
1. Fill in the online form at apply.riverbend.edu.bd.
2. Pay the application fee of 1,500 Taka (by bKash, Nagad or bank card).
3. Upload a passport-size photo and copies of your SSC and HSC certificates.
4. Download your admit card 3 days before the test.
5. Results are published on the website within 5 working days after the test.

After you pass
You must complete admission within 7 days after the result. Bring the original certificates, 2 photos and the admission payment. Students who do not complete admission in 7 days lose their seat.
