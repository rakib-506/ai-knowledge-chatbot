STUDENT HOSTEL GUIDE

Riverbend University has two hostels on the main campus:
- Shapla Hall for female students (320 seats).
- Kopotakkho Hall for male students (450 seats).

Room types and rent (per month)
- Single room: 9,000 Taka.
- Double room (2 students): 6,000 Taka per student.
- Four-bed room (4 students): 4,000 Taka per student.
Rent includes electricity, water and Wi-Fi. Meals are not included.

Meals
The hostel dining hall serves breakfast, lunch and dinner. A monthly meal plan costs 5,500 Taka. Students can also buy single meals with their student ID card.

How to get a seat
Hostel seats are given first to first-year students who live outside Chattogram city. Apply on the student portal within the first 2 weeks of the trimester. A security deposit of 10,000 Taka is needed. The deposit is returned when the student leaves the hostel.

Hostel rules
- Gate closing time is 10:30 PM for both halls.
- Guests are allowed only in the visitor room, from 4:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
- Cooking inside rooms is not allowed.
- Smoking is not allowed anywhere in the hostel.
- Students who break rules 3 times can lose their hostel seat.

Facilities
Each hall has a common TV room, a prayer room, a reading room, laundry machines and a small gym. A hostel supervisor lives in each hall. In an emergency, students can call the hostel supervisor at +880 31 555 0177 (24 hours).
