# About Riverbend University

Riverbend University is a private university in Chattogram, Bangladesh. It was founded in 2004 by the Riverbend Education Trust. The university started with 3 departments and 240 students. Today it has 11 departments, about 7,800 students and 410 teachers.

## Campus
The main campus is at 25 Hillview Road, Khulshi, Chattogram. It covers 18 acres. The campus has 6 academic buildings, a central library, 2 student hostels, a sports field, a medical centre and a cafeteria. The city campus in Agrabad is used only for evening MBA classes.

## Faculties and departments
- Faculty of Science and Engineering: Computer Science and Engineering (CSE), Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE), Civil Engineering, and Mathematics.
- Faculty of Business: Business Administration (BBA), MBA, and Economics.
- Faculty of Arts and Social Sciences: English, Law, Journalism, and Public Health.

## Leadership
The Vice-Chancellor is Professor Nasrin Akhter. She has led the university since 2019. The Registrar is Mr. Tanvir Hossain.

## Academic calendar
The university uses a trimester system. There are three trimesters every year: Spring (January to April), Summer (May to August) and Fall (September to December). Each trimester has 14 weeks of classes and 2 weeks of exams.
