# Exam and Grading Policy

## Exams in a trimester
Each course has:
- Class tests and assignments: 20% of the marks.
- Attendance: 5% of the marks.
- Midterm exam: 30% of the marks.
- Final exam: 45% of the marks.

## Attendance rule
Students must attend at least 70% of the classes of a course. Students below 70% cannot sit for the final exam of that course and get grade F.

## Grading scale
| Marks | Letter grade | Grade point |
|---|---|---|
| 80 and above | A+ | 4.00 |
| 75 to 79 | A | 3.75 |
| 70 to 74 | A- | 3.50 |
| 65 to 69 | B+ | 3.25 |
| 60 to 64 | B | 3.00 |
| 55 to 59 | B- | 2.75 |
| 50 to 54 | C+ | 2.50 |
| 45 to 49 | C | 2.25 |
| 40 to 44 | D | 2.00 |
| Below 40 | F | 0.00 |

## Retake and improvement
- A student who gets F must retake the course. The retake fee is the normal course fee.
- A student who gets C+ or lower can take the course again to improve the grade. The best grade is counted.
- A student can improve at most 4 courses in the whole degree.

## Missed exams
A student who misses the midterm or final exam because of illness can apply for a make-up exam. Apply within 3 working days with a medical certificate. The make-up exam fee is 2,000 Taka per course.

## Probation
A student whose CGPA falls below 2.00 is put on academic probation. A student on probation can take at most 9 credits in the next trimester. A student who stays on probation for 3 trimesters in a row is dismissed from the university.

## Graduation
To graduate, a student needs a CGPA of at least 2.00 and must complete all required credits.
